The wrong question

“Is my English good enough?”

It's what almost every executive asks me in our first conversation. The right question is different: good enough for what? For which room, with whom on the other side, on what date?

Your English isn't behind. Your idea is.

Communication isn't measured when it leaves you. It's measured when it arrives. If the message isn't clear in your own language, it won't arrive whole in English.

The student's trap

The more you study like a student, the more you wait to be corrected.

And students don't negotiate, lead or give talks. Reading, taking notes and reviewing feel like progress. Confidence is born when you speak, record and repeat the real situation.

You're not a student. You're a speaker.

The Inductive Method

Four steps. One scene.

Every process starts with your scene, not with grammar. The four steps work together: remove one and the whole result weakens.

C

Scene

Scene Map

The date, the room, who decides on the other side and what is at stake for you.

E

Essence

Commanding Voice

The idea organized before it becomes English. The same authority in both languages, without the emotional block.

N

Niche

Insider Vocabulary

The language of your world: law, medicine, negotiation, imports. What you won't use stays out.

A

Action

Real Rehearsal

The scene repeated until it becomes memory, with a short daily recording and feedback. No reading, no notes.

In every step: body, voice and emotion. Posture, diction and presence.

How it works

From application to opening night.

  1. Application

    You tell me your scene and the date. It takes about 1 minute.

  2. Business Analysis

    A free conversation with me about the commitment, what's at stake and the right format for you. If it's not the right time, I'll tell you.

  3. Scene Map

    Together we map out the room, the people and the situations you'll face.

  4. Weekly rehearsal

    1 to 5 live sessions a week, depending on your date and your pace, with practice and a short daily recording with feedback.

  5. Opening night

    A full final simulation. When the day comes, you already know the scene.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

My English is basic. Is this for me?

Yes. The method starts from speaking and from your scene, not from grammar. In the Business Analysis we look at where you are and what's possible by your date.

How long does it take?

The timeline is built around your date. I've prepared clients in 45 days and also in 6 months.

I'm short on time. Can I keep up?

The process was designed for a leader's schedule: sessions at a fixed time and short daily recordings, under a minute long.

I've taken courses before and they didn't work. Why would this be different?

Courses teach the language. Here you rehearse your scene, with the people, the questions and the words you'll actually face.

I freeze. I'm embarrassed to make mistakes.

You don't need to lose the fear to get started. The emotional block is worked on in the process, and confidence is born from rehearsal.

I don't have a date set yet.

I work from a real commitment. Tell me your goal in the application: if it's not the right time, I'll tell you honestly.

Is it online? Do you work with people outside Brazil?

Yes. The whole process is online, for clients in Brazil and abroad.

How much does it cost?

The investment depends on the format, from 1 to 5 sessions a week, and is presented in the Business Analysis, once we understand your scene.
